# Sales Performance Analytics and KPI Dashboard

The **Sales Performance Analytics and KPI Dashboard** is a comprehensive data visualization tool designed to track and analyze key sales metrics, profit margins, shipment details, and product performance across multiple regions. The dashboard utilizes advanced charting techniques to provide clear insights into sales trends, helping businesses optimize their decision-making process.

![portfolio-7](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a9e0deff-9c78-4af0-959f-b29e2f244a98)

## Features
- **Stacked Bar Charts** for visualizing total sales, profit, and cost over time.
- **Doughnut Charts** to display total sales and profit by geographic regions.
- **Horizontal Bar Charts** to compare sales performance across different products and regions.
- **Line Charts** for analyzing sales trends by month and year.
- **Gauge Charts** to monitor real-time sales and track KPIs.
  
## Data Sources
- Sales data is categorized by:
  - **People**: Customer demographics and behavior
  - **Shipments**: Shipment volumes and logistics
  - **Calendar**: Monthly and yearly performance
  - **Products**: Breakdown of sales by product category
  - **Locations**: Regional sales distribution

## Technologies Used
- **Power BI ** for data visualization.
- **Python** for backend data processing and analysis.

## Usage
This dashboard can be used by sales teams, financial analysts, and business managers to:
- Track and optimize sales performance.
- Identify key drivers of profit and cost.
- Monitor shipment logistics and product demand by region.
- Predict future trends based on historical data.

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales
- Profit Margins
- Cost Breakdown
- Regional Performance
- Shipment Volumes
- Monthly and Yearly Sales Trends

## Conclusion
The **Sales Performance Analytics and KPI Dashboard** is an essential tool for businesses looking to improve their sales strategies through data-driven insights, facilitating more informed and efficient decision-making.
