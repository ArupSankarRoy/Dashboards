
# Financial Preferences and Savings Goals Dashboard
The **Financial Preferences and Savings Goals Dashboard** is a dynamic, interactive dashboard designed to help users track their financial habits and progress toward savings goals. It provides a clear, data-driven view of key financial metrics, enabling users to monitor their spending, analyze savings trends, and make informed decisions to reach their financial targets efficiently.

![portfolio-9](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2aeb8566-2d01-48cd-b503-c3071b554343)


## Features
- **Savings Overview**: Visual representation of current savings status against set financial goals.
- **Spending Categories**: Pie charts and bar charts for analyzing spending distribution across various categories.
- **Monthly Spending Trends**: Line charts showing monthly spending patterns, highlighting areas for potential savings.
- **Financial Goals Tracking**: Monitoring progress toward individual and aggregate financial goals over time.
- **Key Metrics**: Quick insights into total savings, total expenditures, and budget surplus/deficit.

## Data Breakdown
- **Savings Goals**: Targeted savings amounts and progress tracking.
- **Spending Categories**: Visualization of expenses in categories such as housing, groceries, entertainment, etc.
- **Monthly/Yearly Trends**: Historical view of financial behaviors to help adjust future budgets.
- **Financial Preferences**: Insight into how spending aligns with personal financial priorities and preferences.

## Technologies Used
- **Power BI** for interactive data visualization and dashboard creation.
  
## Usage
The dashboard is ideal for individuals, financial planners, and analysts who want to:
- Track savings goals progress and align spending with their financial preferences.
- Visualize and reduce unnecessary expenses through detailed spending breakdowns.
- Plan budgets more effectively by analyzing historical spending trends.
- Optimize financial decisions by focusing on areas where savings can be maximized.

## Key Metrics
- Total Savings
- Total Expenditures
- Surplus/Deficit Budget
- Monthly Spending by Category
- Progress Towards Savings Goals

## Conclusion
The **Financial Preferences and Savings Goals Dashboard** helps users take control of their financial future by offering actionable insights and personalized visualizations that align with their savings goals and spending preferences.
